Airway and Ventilation
ETT size

Uncuffed ETT size = (Age/4) + 4 [Cole formula]

Cuffed ETT size = (Age/4) + 3.5 [Modified Cole]
ETT depth (oral)

Above 1 year: Depth ≈ Age/2+12

    Neonates
  • Depth ≈ 1.17 × Birth weight (kg) + 5.58 (Tochen)
  • 123–789 rule: 7 cm for a 1 kg baby, 8 cm for a 2 kg baby, and 9 cm for a 3 kg baby at the lips.
ETT depth (nasal)

Above 1 year: Depth ≈ Age/2+15
Circulation
Lowest acceptable SBP

<1 month: ≥60 mmHg

1 month to 1 year: ≥70 mmHg

1–10 years: 2 x Age + 70 mmHg
Lowest acceptable MAP

Age (in years) x 1.5 + 40 mmHg
Premature Neonates

Gestational age ≈ Mean Arterial Pressure
