Transfusion Targets (Standard Adult)
Haemoglobin > 70–80 g/L
Fibrinogen > 1.5 g/L
Platelets > 50 × 109/L
PT / APTT < 1.5× normal

Note: Target restrictive Hb (70–80 g/L) in stable non-bleeding surgical patients, but maintain higher thresholds during ongoing major haemorrhage until bleeding is controlled.

Major Haemorrhage Packs (MHP)
Pack A (Pack 1) 4 PRBC + 4 FFP
(+ 1g IV TXA)

*Tranexamic Acid (TXA): Administer 1 g IV over 10 min as early as possible (strictly within 3 hours of bleeding onset). Follow with a continuous IV infusion of 1 g over 8 hours.

Note: Guide subsequent product administration with laboratory assays or viscoelastometry (ROTEM/TEG). TXA is generally not recommended for severe gastrointestinal bleeding.
