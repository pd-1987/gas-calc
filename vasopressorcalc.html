Hydrocortisone

For adults with septic shock and an ongoing requirement for vasopressor therapy.

200 mg/24 hours: 50 mg IV every 6 hours or a continuous infusion.

Commenced at a dose of noradrenaline or adrenaline ≥0.25 mcg/kg/min at least 4 hours after initiation.

Noradrenaline / Adrenaline / Vasopressin: can be diluted with 0.9% sodium chloride or 5% glucose.
