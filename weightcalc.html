Notes and references
  • Ideal Body Weight (IBW) – Devine formula. For males: 50 kg + 2.3 kg for each inch over 5 ft; For females: 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg for each inch over 5 feet. Not validated for heights below 152.4 cm/5 ft.
  • Adjusted Body Weight: IBW + 0.4 × (Actual weight in kg – IBW).
  • Lean Body Weight – Janmahasatian formula. For males: LBM = (9270 × W) / (6680 + 216 × BMI); For females: LBM = (9270 × W) / (8780 + 244 × BMI).
