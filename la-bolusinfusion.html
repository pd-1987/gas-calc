Notes

Bupivacaine: A maximum dose of 2 mg/kg should not be exceeded in any four-hour period1; not to exceed 175 mg per dose2; In total ≤400 mg/24h.3 Paediatric textbooks advise an upper limit of 0.3 – 0.375 mg/kg/hour (approximately 400 mg/24h in a 45 kg patient).4

Levobupivacaine: Not to exceed 200 mg per dose.2 In total ≤400 mg/24h.3

Ropivacaine: Not to exceed 200 mg per dose.2, 5 In total ≤675 mg/24h.5, 6

IBW: ideal body weight; LBW: lean body weight; BNF: British National Formulary; SOBA: The Society for Obesity and Bariatric Anaesthesia; AoA: Association of Anaesthetists (UK)
